Care team

Who does what around MediCart
  • PhysicianReviews vitals remotely and runs the video consultation from this console.
  • NurseHelps the patient at the cart — positions the finger sensor and the camera.
  • PatientPlaces a fingertip on the MAX30105 sensor and talks to the doctor on screen.
  • MediCartFollows the line to the station, stops for people within 1 m, carries camera, mic, speaker and sensor.
